# Riverbend General Hospital emergency department: talking with waiting families

How patients are seen: every patient is assessed by a triage nurse on arrival and seen by how urgent their condition is, not by arrival time. Red: at once. Orange: within 15 minutes. Yellow: within 60 minutes. Green: within 2 hours.

When a family member asks why their relative is still waiting:
1. Listen first, and say what you see: "You've been waiting a long time and you're worried about your father."
2. Explain in plain words that the sickest patients are seen first, and that their relative has already been assessed.
3. Do not give a medical opinion, a diagnosis or reassurance such as "it's nothing serious". You are not the doctor for this patient.
4. Never promise when the doctor will come. Promise what you control: "I'll come back and update you within 30 minutes."
5. Ask whether anything has changed since the patient was assessed: more pain, trouble breathing, fainting, confusion. If yes, tell the triage nurse at once so the patient is assessed again.
6. If the family member stays upset, offer to fetch the charge nurse. Call security only if someone is threatened.
7. Never argue, and never say "that's the rule" without explaining it.
