Incident report INC-0412 (internal)
Customer: Kumo Logistics (warehouse management platform, Osaka region)
Start: 09:12 local time. Customer impact ended: 11:05.
Impact: warehouse staff could not save new orders. Picking and outbound shipping stopped at three warehouses for about two hours.
Cause: a database schema migration in the morning release locked the orders table. The automatic rollback did not start.
Detection: Kumo's operations team reported it by phone at 09:31. Our monitoring alerted at 09:40.
Fix: release rolled back manually at 10:58. Orders saving again at 11:05.
Next steps: add a lock timeout to migrations; test rollback in staging; send Kumo a written summary within 2 working days.
This is the second incident affecting Kumo this month (the first was a 40-minute login outage on the 3rd).
