# Pebble HR sales playbook (extract)

Product: Pebble HR, leave and payroll software for companies with 50 to 500 staff.
Price: from 3 USD per employee per month, billed yearly. Setup is free for yearly plans.

Discovery first: find at least two pains before showing anything. Typical pains: payroll errors, leave requests on paper or chat, manager approvals lost, end-of-month overtime.

Top objections and how good reps answer:
1. "It is too expensive." Ask what payroll errors and overtime cost them today; compare with 3 USD per employee.
2. "We already use spreadsheets." Ask how long the monthly payroll close takes and who checks it.
3. "Not now." Ask what would make it a priority and agree a date to talk again.

Every call ends with a booked next step: a demo with the HR lead, a trial, or a call with finance.
