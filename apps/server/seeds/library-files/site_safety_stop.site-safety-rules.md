# Bamboo Build site rules: working at height and stopping unsafe work

Working at height: anyone above 2 metres wears a full harness clipped to a marked anchor point at all times. If the nearest anchor is too far, stop and ask the supervisor for a mobile anchor or a lifeline. Never work unclipped "for a minute".
Damaged gear: a frayed lanyard or a cracked buckle is tagged "Do not use" and swapped at the site store the same day. Report it to the site safety officer.

Stop-work: anyone may stop unsafe work, and a supervisor must. When you see it:
1. Stop the task and make the area safe before talking.
2. Talk to the worker in private and calmly. No shouting, no public blame.
3. Describe what you saw and the risk, not the person: "You were unclipped on the edge of level 3."
4. Ask why it happened. Shortcuts usually have a cause: a missing tool, a far anchor, time pressure.
5. Agree one fix for the cause, and who does it by when.
6. Write it in the daily site log and check again before the end of the shift.

Pay: time lost to a safety stop is paid and does not reduce the per-unit bonus. Tell the worker this if pay is the reason for the shortcut.
