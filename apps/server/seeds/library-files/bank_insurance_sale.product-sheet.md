# Lantern Future 10: sales rules for Harbour Bank branch staff

What it is: a life insurance plan with a savings part, made by Lantern Life and sold at Harbour Bank branches. It is not a bank deposit, the bank does not guarantee it, and deposit insurance does not cover it.
Premium: 30 million VND a year, paid every year for 10 years.
Money back if the customer cancels early (share of premiums paid): year 1: 0%; year 2: 40%; year 3: 60%; year 5: 85%; year 10: 105%. Only the year-10 figure is guaranteed.
Cooling-off: the customer can cancel within 21 days of receiving the contract and get all premiums back.

You must say all of these in every sale:
- It is insurance, not savings. Lantern Life is the insurer; Harbour Bank only sells it.
- The yearly premium and the 10-year term.
- Cancelling early returns less than was paid, using the table above.
- Any projected return is not guaranteed.
- Buying it is never a condition for a loan or any other bank service.
- The 21-day cooling-off right.

You must check: what the money is for, whether the customer can pay 30 million VND every year for 10 years, and whether they may need the money within 10 years. If they may, say this plan does not fit.
Never: call it a savings book, promise a return, link it to a loan, fill in the form for the customer, or use another person's agent code. If the customer is unsure, offer to let them take the papers home.
