# Corvenol 10 mg: approved messages for medical representatives

Approved use: treatment of high blood pressure in adults.
Approved claims:
- Corvenol lowers blood pressure in adults with hypertension, as shown in the approved product information.
- Corvenol is taken once a day.
Safety information that must be given whenever benefits are discussed: dizziness and swelling of the ankles are common side effects; Corvenol must not be used in pregnancy.
Not approved: use in children, use for heart failure, any claim that it is better than other medicines.
If a doctor asks about use outside the approved use: say you can only discuss the approved use, and offer to pass the question to the Medical Information team.
