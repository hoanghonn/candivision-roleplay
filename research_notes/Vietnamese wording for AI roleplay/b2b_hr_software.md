# Vietnamese wording in B2B SaaS, HR and corporate learning software

Evidence tiers used throughout. Quotes are verbatim, including source typos and en dashes.
- **T1 - help centre, labels in or describing the real UI:** Gitiho docs (the single best source found), Base help, ClassIn guide, Odoo VI docs.
- **T2 - vendor product or sales page:** Gitiho Business, FPT.eLearning, SmartLMS, Lạc Việt, 1Office, MISA, FastWork, Edubit.
- **T3 - blog or job listing:** MISA and 1Office articles, Joboko, CareerViet.
- Odoo is a Belgian product and ClassIn is not a Vietnamese vendor. Treat both as supporting evidence, never as the sole basis for a choice.

## How do these products word the core training terms?

### Takeaway
The working vocabulary is consistent: "khóa học", "học viên" or "người học", "nhân sự" when an admin refers to staff, "Admin / Quản trị viên", "tiến độ", "chứng chỉ", "Đạt / Không đạt", "điều kiện hoàn thành", "xuất bản", "nháp", "cho phép làm lại". Vendors do not use "team" for reporting; they report by "phòng ban". "Khung đánh giá" and "tiêu chí bắt buộc" were not found in any source read.

### Inferences
- Gitiho's split looks deliberate and worth copying: "nhân sự" when the admin is managing people, "học viên" when the person is learning, "thí sinh" only during a formal exam.
- "Đạt / Không đạt" is the product label for pass and fail. Softer forms such as "chưa đáp ứng yêu cầu" exist, but appear in prose for managers, not as a state label.
- Reports are organised by "cá nhân, phòng ban hoặc toàn công ty", so "báo cáo theo phòng ban" fits the market better than a translated "team report". This is inferred from the MISA and Gitiho wording; the exact phrase "báo cáo theo phòng ban" was not quoted from a source.
- For a rubric, "tiêu chí đánh giá" is the safe term. "Thang điểm" should only mean the scale. "Khung năng lực" is a different concept (competency framework), and HR staff are likely to read it that way.

### Gaps
- **"khung đánh giá":** zero hits in every page downloaded. Not verified as market wording.
- **"tiêu chí bắt buộc":** not found as a phrase. "Bắt buộc" is verified only on programmes and rules, so its use on criteria is unverified.
- **"chưa đạt":** zero hits in the pages read.
- **"Private workspace":** no verified equivalent. Nearest strings are "Góc học tập" ([Gitiho](https://docs.gitiho.com/danh-cho-hoc-vien/hdsd-goc-hoc-tap-danh-cho-hoc-vien), a learner's own area), "không gian học tập" ([FPT.eLearning](https://fpt-is.com/elearning/)) and the opposite "Mở công khai" ([Gitiho](https://docs.gitiho.com/module-testbank/dot-thi/quan-ly-danh-sach-cac-dot-thi)). - Most vendor pages other than Gitiho are marketing or articles, so their terms are not proof of on-screen labels.

## What is the common Vietnamese job title for the person who runs internal training?

### Takeaway
Two titles dominate, usually paired with the English in brackets: "Chuyên viên Đào tạo" or "Chuyên viên L&D" at staff level, and "Trưởng phòng Đào tạo (L&D Manager)" at lead level. The function is usually described as sitting in "phòng Nhân sự" or a "bộ phận Đào tạo & Phát triển (L&D)".

## What is Candivision's existing Vietnamese voice?

### Takeaway
Candivision is plain, warm and second-person ("bạn"), with short benefit-led sentences and careful failure copy. Its core nouns fit a recruiting and interview product ("thành viên", "mẫu phỏng vấn", "phỏng vấn thử"), so a training-lead product would need to re-map them. Three issues stand out: a B2C tone leaking into the company product, "điểm" meaning both score and credit, and inconsistent spelling.

**Tone, quoted**
- Pain-led and plain: "Không biết ai đã sẵn sàng"; "Được giới thiệu rồi vẫn trượt"; "Bạn có đang gặp những điều này?" - `landing-b2c.json`
- Conviction: "Chất lượng bắt đầu từ sự chuẩn bị" - `landing-odc.json`; "Tôi tin rằng một cuộc phỏng vấn tốt nên bắt đầu từ sự chuẩn bị, không phải ngồi cầu sự may mắn." - `landing-odc.json`
- Honest about AI: "chúng tôi xem đây là công cụ hỗ trợ và không phải công cụ phán quyết" - `landing-odc.json`
- Reassuring in-product: "Đây chỉ là không gian luyện tập. Hãy hít một hơi thật sâu" - `practice.json`; "Bạn đang đi đúng hướng" - `interview.json`
- Takes responsibility: "Đã có lỗi từ phía chúng tôi." - `interview.json`
- Casual B2C: "Cùng mình nâng cấp hồ sơ ứng tuyển của bạn nhé ~"; "Tập phỏng vấn trước khi \"ra trận\""; "để \"soi\" CV" - `dashboard.json`; star labels with emoji such as "Tuyệt vời 🤩" - `interview.json`
- Formal outlier: "Quý khách hàng đã thiết lập xong." - `dashboard.json` `setup_checklist.all_done`

**Issues seen in the files**
- "điểm" carries two meanings: credit ("Số điểm còn lại", "Điểm đã dùng") and score ("Điểm số"). The two are side by side in `practice.json` (`history_col_score`, `history_col_credit`).
- Mixed spelling: "Xoá bộ lọc" (`interview.json`) and "Xóa bộ lọc" (`practice.json`); "Huỷ" and "Hủy"; "tuỳ chỉnh" and "tùy chỉnh". Vendors are mixed too ("khóa học", "khoá học"), but within one product it reads as inconsistent.
- Literal or stiff strings: "Đi" (`dashboard.json` `student.go`), "fire" (`gamification.fire`), "Thử phỏng vấn thử đầu tiên" (`suggestion_first_interview`).
- Pass and fail words: no "Đạt / Không đạt" label exists. Marketing uses "đậu phỏng vấn" and "trượt" (`landing-b2c.json`).
- Publish words: no "xuất bản" or "nháp". Status is "Mở" / "Đóng", "Đang hoạt động" / "Đã đóng" (`interview.json`).

### Inferences
- Already market-aligned and worth keeping: "tiêu chí đánh giá", "báo cáo", "Admin", "tư vấn", the "Không thể X. Vui lòng thử lại." error pattern, and the honest "công cụ hỗ trợ" position on AI.
- Likely to need re-mapping for a training lead, based on the vendor wording above: "thành viên" (vendors use "nhân sự" in admin views and "học viên" or "người học" for learners); "mẫu phỏng vấn" (no training equivalent is verified for a roleplay template, and vendors' nearest objects are "khóa học", "chương trình đào tạo", "bài kiểm tra"); the "Mở / Đóng" status where publish behaviour is meant ("Xuất bản", "nháp").
- The casual tone ("nhé ~", "ra trận", emoji) fits a job seeker, but no B2B vendor read uses this register for admins. Vendor copy addressing admins is neutral and procedural, while Candivision's warmth is an asset for learner screens.
- If credits stay in the product, "điểm" should be separated from scores, because "Đạt", "mức điểm đạt" and "điểm" will all sit on the same screens. No alternative word for credit was verified in this research, so the choice remains open.

### Gaps
- These five files do not include `admin.json`, `members.json`, `billing.json` or `onboarding.json`, which may hold more B2B wording. They were outside the brief and were not read.
- Whether Vietnamese HR staff perceive this tone as too casual is untested; I found no user research on it.
