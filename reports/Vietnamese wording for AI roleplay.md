# Write Vietnamese That Trainers Already Speak

Rewrite `vi.json` as a term swap plus a sentence pass, not a fresh translation, because the market's vocabulary is small and the file is already close to it. Vietnamese products and trainers converge on "luyện tập", "nhập vai", "tình huống", "phản hồi", "bộ tiêu chí", "Đạt", "học viên" and "Báo cáo học tập", with bare-verb buttons and "bạn" as the form of address. The largest changes are to drop "giám khảo" (17 of 402 strings), "cuộc trò chuyện khó", "Báo cáo nhóm", "kiểm chứng" and the "ngân sách ... giờ UTC" messages, and to adopt "phản hồi", the market's default word for feedback, which appears in no string today. Sales copy should lead with the practical result and a demo or consultation offer, then use research as the reason to believe, because buyers already distrust AI output: only 5.4% of surveyed firms strongly trust it ([ITviec](https://itviec.com/blog/tom-tat-chinh-bao-cao-ung-dung-ai-va-tuyen-dung-it-tai-vietnam/)). Evidence is uneven. No A/B test, conversion data or adult-learner reaction study exists in this research, and three recommended wordings ("Chưa đạt" as a product label, "Nhấn giữ để nói", "hai AI chấm") have no product precedent. Every rewritten string below is a draft for a native-speaker check. Two issues must clear before release: `account.workspaceText` makes a privacy promise that the team report contradicts, and Decision 33/2026 needs a lawyer's reading.

## The market shares a small plain vocabulary, split by reader

AI speaking apps supply the learner's vocabulary. Duolingo's Vietnamese web bundle, the only large set of real interface strings available, uses "Luyện nói", "Luyện tập", "Bắt đầu", "Tiếp tục", "Thử lại", "Phản hồi" and "Nhập vai" ([Duolingo UI](https://d35aaqx5ub95lt.cloudfront.net/js/9036-5fdcdec5.js)). ELSA's help page describes "cuộc hội thoại nhập vai" and "bối cảnh nhập vai", and tells users "Nút micro: Bấm vào để bắt đầu trò chuyện" ([ELSA help](https://vn.elsaspeak.com/cau-hoi-thuong-gap/)). PrepTalk sells "Phòng ảo nhập vai Prep AI" ([PrepTalk](https://prepedu.com/vi/prep-talk-english)). **"Nhập vai" names the mode, while "đóng vai" describes what a party does**, as in "Gemini Edu đóng vai nhà tuyển dụng" ([MoMo](https://www.momo.vn/sinh-vien/blog/luyen-phong-van-bang-ai-5204)). Coaches split by vendor between "gia sư" and "huấn luyện viên", but the product's AI plays a customer, not a coach, so neither fits the character. These apps mostly teach English, and most in-app screens sat behind logins, so apart from Duolingo the wording here is marketing or help text.

B2B learning software supplies the training lead's vocabulary, and Gitiho's help centre is the strongest source. It switches noun by context: "nhân sự" when an admin manages people, "học viên" when the person is learning, and "thí sinh" only in a formal exam ([Gitiho, nhân sự](https://docs.gitiho.com/cau-hoi-thuong-gap/co-the-reset-lai-ket-qua-hoc-tap-cua-nhan-su-khong), [Gitiho, học viên](https://docs.gitiho.com/danh-cho-hoc-vien/hdsd-goc-hoc-tap-danh-cho-hoc-vien)). Its pass labels are "Đạt" and "Không đạt", with "Cho phép làm lại" for retakes ([Gitiho, đợt thi](https://docs.gitiho.com/module-testbank/dot-thi/quan-ly-danh-sach-cac-dot-thi)). Reports are "Báo cáo học tập", read by "tên và phòng ban" ([Gitiho, báo cáo](https://docs.gitiho.com/module-lms/bao-cao-hoc-tap/bao-cao-tong-quan-hoc-tap)). **No vendor read used "team" or "nhóm" as the reporting unit, and none used "khung đánh giá" or "tiêu chí bắt buộc".** English stays normal for KPI, LMS, L&D, HR, Admin, demo and onboarding, yet a whole-word check of 8 vendor pages found no "feedback" at all ([1Office](https://1office.vn/phan-mem-quan-ly-dao-tao), [Gitiho Business](https://business.gitiho.com/)).

Corporate trainers and call-centre QA supply the scenario vocabulary, and they name situations by event or customer type rather than by abstract skill. Among 66 saved pages, "tình huống thực tế" appeared on 14, "xử lý từ chối" on 11, "khách hàng khó tính" on 8 and "thực chiến" on 13, while "sắm vai", "tập dượt" and "giám khảo" appeared on none. These are raw counts from a convenience sample, so they show direction, not market share. HRD Academy writes "Role Play: Thực hành theo tình huống tại Đơn vị" ([HRD](https://hrd.com.vn/ky-nang-ban-hang-b2b-chuyen-nghiep)), one script labels a case "Khách nói giá cao" ([Phần mềm MKT](https://phanmemmkt.vn/mau-kich-ban-cham-soc-khach-hang-qua-dien-thoai)), and QA writers use "Bộ tiêu chí (scorecard)" ([Bell24](https://bell24vietnam.vn/en/van-hanh/criteria-for-evaluating-call-quality/)). The premium names (Dale Carnegie, BizLIGHT, VinUni, Mi Group, PACE) were blocked or empty, so this picture leans on OCD, HRD, iRTC, IFA, PMS and software vendors, and it shows how sellers write, not what trainers say in class.

## Term decisions cover what the product actually says

Evidence labels are strict. **Verified UI** means a real interface string or a help page describing the interface. **Verified copy** means vendor marketing, course pages or job ads, which prove usage but not an on-screen label. **Partial** means the words are verified but this combination or use is not. **Inference** means no market source supports it. Partial and Inference rows need a native-speaker check. One-source and foreign-vendor limits are noted where they matter.

Three existing choices are worth defending against an over-eager rewrite. "Chưa đạt" stays because "chưa" implies the learner can still get there and Microsoft distinguishes "không" from "chưa" ([MS p.41](https://download.microsoft.com/download/b/f/e/bfecb1b4-21ab-48fd-a48c-c2471b026f8f/vie-vnm-StyleGuide.pdf)), but it belongs on a criterion and not on the person. The school reading is a real risk for adults, and no evidence on adult reactions exists. "Đánh giá" stays where a technical term is needed, but two vendors name "bị đánh giá" as the fear they remove ([Praktika](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.praktika.android&hl=vi&gl=VN), [ELSA help](https://vn.elsaspeak.com/cau-hoi-thuong-gap/)), so it should not head a learner screen. "Đánh giá hiệu suất" stays in the footer because it is the market's own phrase ([Gitiho Business](https://business.gitiho.com/)).

## Spoken, short sentences and "bạn" set the style standard

Microsoft's Vietnamese style guide is the only public software authority found, and its instruction is to rewrite from intent, in clear, friendly, everyday language, so the product looks originally written in Vietnamese ([MS pp.6, 43](https://download.microsoft.com/download/b/f/e/bfecb1b4-21ab-48fd-a48c-c2471b026f8f/vie-vnm-StyleGuide.pdf)). No public Apple or Google prose guide surfaced, and no writing guidance from MoMo, VNG, Zalo, Tiki or Shopee was found. The best local practitioner is Thùy Dương's newsletter ([Dương](https://greatuxwriting.substack.com/p/9-meo-cai-thien-noi-dung-ux)).

**Form of address.** Use "bạn" or no pronoun in all UI. Microsoft bans generic "anh" and "chị" ([MS p.16](https://download.microsoft.com/download/b/f/e/bfecb1b4-21ab-48fd-a48c-c2471b026f8f/vie-vnm-StyleGuide.pdf)), and the sampled apps have zero "anh/chị" for the learner. Use "tôi" only on the user's own controls and consent, so "Cuộc gọi của tôi" stays. Refer to a learner by name or role in the lead's views. Persona dialogue is a separate register: FACTS.md shows characters using "chị" and "em", which is right in a scene, and no source addresses this split, so a native reviewer should confirm it.

**Tone splits by screen.** Vendor copy addressing admins is neutral and procedural. Learner screens can be warmer, and a single "nhé" fits a retry prompt. The casual Candivision lines ("nhé ~", "ra trận", emoji) and Duolingo's proverbs suit consumers, not employees at work ([Duolingo UI](https://d35aaqx5ub95lt.cloudfront.net/js/9036-5fdcdec5.js)). This split is inference; no source tests it.

**Loanwords.** Keep AI, CV, KPI, HR, email, demo and acronyms ([MS p.20](https://download.microsoft.com/download/b/f/e/bfecb1b4-21ab-48fd-a48c-c2471b026f8f/vie-vnm-StyleGuide.pdf), [1Office](https://1office.vn/)). L&D belongs in buyer copy only. Translate feedback, team, role-play and persona. Prep's heavy mixing ("band", "Submit", "transcript") suits IELTS students and is a poor model for employees.

## Buyers want a result they can defend; learners want an honest safe space

No survey ranks what Vietnamese training buyers care about, so any priority order is judgement. The usable signal is what vendors put first and what buyers doubt. MISA leads with customer counts, renewal rate and security badges, and Unica with "ĐƯỢC TIN DÙNG BỞI 1000+ DOANH NGHIỆP" and named logos ([MISA](https://amis.misa.vn/ld/giai-phap-quan-tri-kenh-phan-phoi-2in1), [Unica](https://lms.unica.vn/)). Those are seller choices, unaudited, and MISA's own counts vary between pages. On the doubt side, **only 5.4% of firms strongly trust AI output**, and in the Talentnet and VNHR Club study, 61% of HR-heavy respondents call AI "con dao hai lưỡi" and only just over half feel it really delivers ([ITviec](https://itviec.com/blog/tom-tat-chinh-bao-cao-ung-dung-ai-va-tuyen-dung-it-tai-vietnam/), [Doanh nghiệp và Hội nhập](https://doanhnghiephoinhap.vn/doanh-nghiep-viet-chi-14-ung-dung-ai-hieu-qua-118958.html)). Workers themselves are heavy users, with 83% using AI at work ([VnEconomy, PwC](https://vneconomy.vn/lao-dong-viet-nam-su-dung-ai-vuot-xa-muc-trung-binh-toan-cau.htm)). So copy should not argue that AI is acceptable. It should show why this AI's output can be checked.

The pain to name already has market language. Gitiho writes "Nhân sự học xong nhưng không thay đổi cách làm việc." and "Không đo được tác động thực sự tới hiệu suất." ([Gitiho Business](https://business.gitiho.com/)). Tanca writes "Chưa có LMS hoặc LMS chỉ lưu file, không đo kỹ năng." ([Tanca](https://tanca.io/baocao2026/Tanca_Vietnam_Corporate_Learning_Report_2026.pdf)). The research notes disagree on how common this is: one sample found almost no pain statements, another found them on Gitiho, 1Office and SmartLMS. The reconciliation is that pain usually appears as a second-screen section rather than a hero headline. Gitiho also lists "AI Coaching", which makes it the competitor to watch on this wording. DeepBrain has a Vietnamese AI role-play page with visibly untranslated lines ([DeepBrain](https://www.aistudios.com/vi/ai-roleplay)). One search found no Vietnamese-built platform, which is weak evidence, but it leaves natural Vietnamese open as a differentiator.

Trainers sell practice share and realism: "80% thời lượng dành cho thực hành" and "Khoá học chung không giải được bài toán riêng" ([PMS](https://pms.edu.vn/dao-tao-inhouse-doanh-nghiep/)). They also promise revenue, as in "tăng 2-5 lần doanh số" ([Unica](https://unica.vn/huan-luyen-nhom-kinh-doanh-dao-tao-doi-sales-tang-2-5-lan-doanh-so)). None of these figures is independently verified, and one testimonial on [kynang.edu.vn](https://www.kynang.edu.vn/ky-nang-nghe-nghiep/ky-nang-cham-soc-khach-hang/15488-khoa-hoc-dao-tao-cham-soc-khach-hang.html) appears misattributed, so do not copy that style. The product's honest edge is the first-try against latest-try comparison on one frozen version, and `team.note` already admits it measures learning, not job results. Research works as a reason and not as a headline: none of the B2B pages read led with it, while Tanca uses foreign data freely when tied to a business outcome. Name the behaviour, then the source, then the human check. Without local references, the honest substitute is a demo on the buyer's own scenario. Do not imply customers that do not exist. The first two or three named pilots with one measured number are worth more than any headline here.

Learners fear people more than machines. A learner on Prep says "Học với PrepTalk đỡ bị tâm lý, thấy tự tin nói hơn" ([Prep](https://prepedu.com/vi)), and Hoang (2025) found a large speaking gain (d = 1.11) among 240 Vietnamese university students using AI tools ([AJET](https://ajet.org.au/index.php/AJET/article/download/10050/2185/34379)). That study covers students, not employees, and the "safe space" reading is the author's interpretation rather than a measured result. Whether staff believe a "private" claim on an employer-paid tool is unknown and is probably the key objection. ## Two flags must clear before these words ship

### Privacy copy must match what the product does

**The team report shows each learner's name, status and number of full calls to the training lead, plus per-skill first-try and latest counts** (`packages/core/src/report.ts`, `candivision-roleplay/src/pages/Dashboard.tsx`). No transcript travels in that payload. Today's no-login build scopes the report to one browser workspace (`callsForVersion` in `apps/server/src/store.ts`), so the promise below holds only in the demo and becomes false once a company lead sees staff. How a lead settles a disagreed line across staff accounts is not yet built, so nobody should promise that leads never see call content. Files also reach the AI model and can be quoted back in debriefs (`apps/server/src/builder.ts`, `packages/core/src/debrief.ts`), and calls go to the Meta and Baseten judges and voice services named in the README. These strings need correcting in both languages.

The recommended stance is to say exactly what the lead sees. Employees who later discover the report will trust nothing else on the page, and buyers gain a sentence they can repeat to staff. "Luyện riêng một mình với nhân vật AI, không có đồng nghiệp hay cấp trên trong phòng" is safe only if placed beside the sentence on what the lead sees. "Không bán dữ liệu" and data-location claims stay out until they are confirmed for the product and its AI providers. A one-page data and use-limits sheet would also serve buyers, since only 7% of firms plan to strengthen AI governance ([ITviec](https://itviec.com/blog/tom-tat-chinh-bao-cao-ung-dung-ai-va-tuyen-dung-it-tai-vietnam/)).

### Decision 33/2026 needs counsel, not clever wording

Decision 33/2026 took effect on 15 August 2026 and lists 46 high-risk AI systems across six sectors, including education. Its education items include systems that "automatically conduct examinations, assess learning outcomes or rank learners" and systems that monitor learner behaviour using biometric or other automated monitoring. Existing education systems must comply by 1 September 2027. Labour and HR are not named sectors, and the summary does not say whether corporate training is covered ([Allen & Gledhill](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/sg/publication/articles/33374/identifies-high-risk-ai-systems-across-six-sectors)). The Vietnamese wording is "hệ thống tự động kiểm tra, đánh giá kết quả, xếp hạng người học" and "hệ thống AI giám sát, phân tích hành vi người học" ([VOV](https://vov.vn/cong-nghe/ban-hanh-danh-muc-he-thong-ai-rui-ro-cao-trong-6-linh-vuc-post1311806.vov)). The original text was not read, so scope is an open question, and what follows is one reading of secondary summaries, not legal advice.

The list is a set of alternatives. Refusing to rank removes one limb only, because two AI models still automatically assess a named learner's result, and a voice product should check the monitoring item too. "Không xếp hạng" is true and a good selling point, but it does not settle the question. Wording cannot escape classification, since features decide it, so do not choose "nhận xét" over "chấm điểm" as a legal strategy. Copy can avoid overclaiming, though: avoid "giám sát", "kỳ thi" and "chứng chỉ do AI cấp", which is one more reason for "Đào tạo đại lý" over "Chứng nhận đối tác". Corporate certificates in this market mostly attach to attendance or completion ([Vân Nguyên](https://vannguyen.edu.vn/khoa-hoc-ky-nang-ban-hang/)), so an AI-gated certificate would be both novel and more exposed. Two neighbouring laws belong in the same review: the Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025, in force since 1 January 2026, restricts sensitive employee data, and AI Law 134/2025 has been in force since 1 March 2026 ([LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/nhung-du-lieu-doanh-nghiep-khong-duoc-thu-thap-khi-tuyen-dung-quan-ly-nhan-su-a169304.html), [Apolat](https://apolatlegal.com/vi/laws/luat-tri-tue-nhan-tao-2025-so-134-2025-qh15/)). Ask counsel three things: does workplace training fall under the education items, does automated pass or not-yet on named staff count, and does voice analysis trigger the monitoring item.

## Conclusion

The file's weakness is narrower than it looks. Most of the translated feel comes from a dozen recurring habits and one register error: an exam word, "giám khảo", sits on what should feel like a practice room. The deliberate departures matter more than the fixes. "Chưa đạt" breaks from Gitiho's "Không đạt", "Hai AI chấm" breaks from Prep's "giám khảo AI", and "Báo cáo học tập" drops a word the product team naturally reaches for. Each should be a recorded decision, so a later translator does not "correct" it back to the market or to the English.

The more surprising result is that the strongest persuasion available is plain disclosure. In a market where nearly every seller shows logos and bold claims, and where buyers already review AI output by hand, "here is exactly what your lead sees, here is the source of every line, and here is where the two AIs disagreed" is both the honest copy and the distinctive copy. It also costs nothing to test, and a native review plus a 5-to-8 person reader check can run before the first pilot.
