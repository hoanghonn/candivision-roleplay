# Industry pain points for ready-built programmes

Research run on 2 November 2026 to choose new ready-built programmes. Six research agents covered six industry groups, preferring Vietnam and Southeast Asia evidence. The figures marked "checked" were re-read at the source before building; the rest are as the agents reported them.

## Built

| Programme | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Selling insurance at a bank counter without misleading (`bank_insurance_sale`) | Checked: a 2023 Ministry of Finance inspection of four life insurers found failures to advise customers directly and 21 to 44 violation cases each; first-year cancellation in the bank channel was up to 70% at many insurers ([Viet Nam News, 10 Jul 2023](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/business-beat/1550893/finance-ministry-unveils-wrongdoings-of-insurance-companies.html)). |
| Stopping a scam at the bank counter (`bank_scam_stop`) | Checked: in March 2026 a bank officer in Quang Tri talked a customer out of paying a fee for a promised US$50,000 transfer and called police; the customer had lost VND100 million to a similar scam ([Tuoi Tre News](https://news.tuoitre.vn/bank-staff-police-foil-50000-overseas-transfer-scam-in-north-central-vietnam-103260329084249856.htm)). Individual cases, not statistics. |
| A site foreman stopping an unsafe shortcut (`site_safety_stop`) | Checked: 8,286 workplace accidents and 727 deaths in 2024, over VND43,000 billion in losses; workers breaking procedures or not using protective equipment caused 22.88% of fatal accidents, and employer failures such as missing training caused 46.91% ([VnEconomy, 26 Feb 2025, citing the labour ministry](https://vneconomy.vn/tai-nan-lao-dong-gay-thiet-hai-hon-43-000-ty-dong-trong-nam-2024.htm)). That a supervisor's conversation prevents accidents is our inference. |
| Explaining an emergency-department wait to a worried relative (`er_wait_relative`) | Checked: 70% of victims of violence against health workers are doctors and 15% nurses, per a Ministry of Health survey; relatives' anxiety, misunderstanding of triage and staff communication are named by experts in the same article, not by the survey ([VnExpress, 9 May 2025](https://vnexpress.net/nan-bao-hanh-blouse-trang-4881673.html)). |

## Runners-up

- **Electricity bill-spike calls:** about 3.2 million of 31.8 million households saw usage rise 30% or more in August 2025 ([Tuoi Tre](https://tuoitre.vn/nguoi-dung-keu-hoa-don-dien-tang-dot-bien-evn-noi-chua-co-sai-sot-nhu-phan-anh-20250910120938646.htm), not checked). Overlaps the refund-complaint programme.
- **Airline delay refunds:** a July 2026 rule sets refund deadlines and minimum compensation ([Tuoi Tre News](https://news.tuoitre.vn/vietnam-sets-air-ticket-refund-deadlines-for-delayed-canceled-flights-103260708154850006.htm), not checked). Needs the official text before building.
- **Telling a team member their raise is delayed:** 93% of employers in a 200-person survey saw more turnover or disengagement from delayed raises ([Bizhub](https://bizhub.vietnamnews.vn/delaying-pay-rises-fuels-staff-turnover-and-disengagement-survey-post381169.html), not checked). Close to the line on personnel decisions.
- **SIM biometric re-verification:** new rule from June 2026 ([Znews](https://znews.vn/sim-bi-khoa-tu-156-neu-bo-qua-buoc-nay-tren-vneid-post1656529.html), not checked). Facts may still change.
- **Nurse discharge teaching with teach-back:** supported by a systematic review ([Yen and Leasure 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6590951)), but no Vietnam evidence.
- **First overdue-payment call:** harassment documented in Vietnam ([ISEAS 2021](https://www.iseas.edu.sg/articles-commentaries/iseas-perspective/2021-122-the-ambivalence-of-heavy-handed-debt-collection-in-vietnam-by-nicolas-lainez-bui-thi-thu-doai-trinh-phan-khanh-le-to-linh-to-thu-phuong-and-emmanuel-pannier/)); the legal status of collection services is unclear.
- **Teacher and upset parent:** over 70% of about 12,500 teachers in three provinces named parents as their biggest pressure ([VietNamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/nguoi-thay-hien-dai-vua-co-don-vua-chiu-qua-nhieu-ap-luc-2343250.html), not checked).

## Dropped

B2B renewals (no primary evidence, close to sales onboarding), marketplace seller account locks (small sample, close to the complaint programme), property handover disputes (disputes are about funds, not calls) and tourist bill disputes (opinion pieces only).

## Not found

Current Vietnamese regulator complaint counts for bancassurance, collections or telecom call quality; a verified national scam-loss total; any study of which words stop a scam victim; Vietnam data linking supervisor conversations to accidents.
